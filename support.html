Slipstream Support

Slipstream is a single-player endless runner for iPhone.

Quick fixes

  • If music is silent, turn Sound on in Settings, raise the device volume, and tap the game once to activate audio.
  • If the game is paused after switching apps, use Resume to continue the current run.
  • Slipstream is landscape-only. Rotate the device sideways if the browser orientation interlock appears.
  • Progress is stored on the device. Deleting the app also deletes local progress.
  • Version 1.0 is ad-free and can be played without a network connection.

Slipstream Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

Slipstream 1.0 is an offline-capable, single-player game. It contains no advertising SDK, account system, in-app purchases, or developer-operated analytics service.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress and preferences—such as scores, earned credits, unlocks, sound settings, and haptic settings—locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or a third party.

Data collection and tracking

The developer does not collect personal data, transmit gameplay data to a developer server, use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, or track activity across other companies' apps and websites in version 1.0.

Accounts and purchases

Version 1.0 has no account system or in-app purchases.

Deletion

Deleting Slipstream removes its locally stored game data, subject to the normal behavior of device backups managed by Apple and the user.

Children

Slipstream is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Privacy policy
Your progress stays on your device.

Slipstream stores progress and settings locally. It may create a random install ID and send gameplay, performance, and feature-experiment events when a secure developer analytics endpoint is configured.

There are no accounts, advertising IDs, location or contact collection, or cross-app tracking. Deleting the app clears its local progress and analytics queue. Effective August 27, 2026.
